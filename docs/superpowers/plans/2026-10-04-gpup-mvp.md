## Review focus

- SSE chunks must never silently become output tokens.
- API keys and prompts must not be persisted or logged.
- Cancellation and duration bounds must reach streaming requests.
- Missing GPU metrics must remain unavailable.
- Benchmarks with errors or incompatible workloads must not pass CI gates.
